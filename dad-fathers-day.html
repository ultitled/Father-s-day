✦ a letter, just for you

a letter i wrote you

21-06-2026
Dear Dad, Happy Father's Day. I have already written a birthday letter, but I felt that wasn't enough because there are some things that belong only in a Father's Day letter. Today is not about achievements, marks, dreams, or goals. Today is about you. I don't think I say this often enough, but thank you for being my dad. Thank you for staying. Thank you for showing up every day. Thank you for carrying responsibilities that I probably don't even know about. Thank you for all the sacrifices you made quietly without expecting anything in return. When I look back at my life, I realize that many of the things I have today are because of your hard work. Many opportunities I got were because you made them possible. Many of the lessons I learned came from watching you, even when you weren't trying to teach me anything. One of the things I admire most about you is your strength. Not the strength people usually talk about. The strength to keep going when life gets difficult. The strength to carry responsibilities. The strength to put your family before yourself. The strength to wake up the next day and continue moving forward no matter what happened yesterday. I have learned a lot from you. I learned that age should never stop someone from learning. I learned that people should ask questions when they want answers. I learned that it is okay to try something new even if others don't understand it. I learned that courage isn't about never being afraid; sometimes it is simply about moving forward anyway. I know we don't always agree on everything. I know we argue. I know I can be stubborn. I know I sometimes speak strongly when I want my opinion to be heard. But even in those moments, one thing never changes. I love you. And I respect you. You have been with me through every stage of my life. Through my successes. Through my failures. Through my confusion. Through my dreams. Through every version of me. And for that, I will always be grateful. There is something else I want you to know. Even when I don't say it, I notice the things you do. I notice the effort. I notice the sacrifices. I notice the care. Sometimes children don't realize these things when they are young. But as I grow older, I understand more and more. I understand that love is not always spoken. Sometimes it is shown through hard work. Sometimes it is shown through responsibility. Sometimes it is shown through simply being there. And you have always been there. Dad, please take care of yourself. You spend so much time thinking about everyone else that you forget about your own health and happiness. I want you to take care of yourself the same way you take care of the people you love. I want many more years of your stories. Many more years of your advice. Many more years of hearing about the crazy things you did when you were my age. And many more years of arguing with you about random things. As I grow older, I hope I carry some of your qualities with me. Your determination. Your courage. Your willingness to keep learning. Your ability to keep moving forward. And your strength. I don't know where life will take me. But I do know that a part of who I am today exists because of you. Thank you for being my father. Thank you for supporting me. Thank you for believing in me. Thank you for helping me become the person I am becoming. And thank you for loving me in all the ways that matter. Happy Father's Day. With all my love, Your daughter
Love you to the moon and back
